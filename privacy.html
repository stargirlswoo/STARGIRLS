STARGIRLS OFFICIAL STORE // POLICY

PRIVACY

Last updated September 6, 2026.

What we collect

When you use the STARGIRLS website or store, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, shipping and billing details, order information, messages, and signup preferences. We may also receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, pages viewed and referral information.

Payments

Payment information is processed by our payment service providers. STARGIRLS does not need to store your full card number on this website. Payment providers may process your information under their own privacy policies.

How we use information

We may use information to process and fulfill orders, provide customer support, send transactional messages, manage signups, protect the store against fraud or abuse, improve the website, understand site performance, and comply with legal obligations.

Service providers

We may share information with vendors that help operate the store, including payment processors, shipping carriers, fulfillment partners, hosting providers, analytics providers and email services. We limit sharing to what is reasonably necessary for those services.

Cookies and analytics

The website may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for functions such as remembering a shopping cart, measuring site performance and improving the experience. Your browser may allow you to delete or restrict these technologies.

Marketing

If you opt in to promotional messages, you may unsubscribe using the method provided in those messages. Transactional order communications may still be sent when necessary to complete or service an order.

Data choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning access, correction or deletion of certain personal information. Requests can be made through the official STARGIRLS website or official social channels so we can direct you to the appropriate support process.

Children

The store is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us through our official channels.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the store, vendors or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

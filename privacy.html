OUR PRIVACY POLICY

This Privacy Policy explains how STARGIRLS ("STARGIRLS," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, interact with our content or use features available through the STARGIRLS website.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you choose to join our mailing list, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, such as your email address and, where available, your name, country or other information you choose to submit.

Our website and the services we use to operate it may also process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages and information about how the website is accessed.

HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We may use information you provide to send STARGIRLS news, music updates, release announcements, videos, behind-the-scenes updates, product news, early access opportunities and other communications from our world.

We may also use information to operate, maintain, improve and protect our website, understand how visitors interact with it and respond to messages or requests.

EMAILS + STAY CLOSE

If you enter your email address through a STAY CLOSE or other sign-up form on our website, you are requesting to receive email communications from STARGIRLS.

We use Brevo to help manage our mailing list and deliver email communications. Information submitted through our mailing-list forms may therefore be processed by Brevo on our behalf in order to provide those services.

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time by using the unsubscribe option included in our emails. Unsubscribing from marketing communications does not necessarily require us to delete information that we are permitted or required to retain for legitimate administrative, security or legal purposes.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Our website may use or link to services provided by third parties. This can include Brevo for mailing-list services, YouTube for videos, Spotify for embedded music players, social-media platforms and website hosting or technical service providers.

These third parties may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies. STARGIRLS does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or platforms.

COOKIES + EMBEDDED CONTENT

Your use of embedded third-party services may also be subject to the privacy and cookie practices of those providers.

HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including maintaining our mailing list, providing requested communications, protecting our services and meeting applicable legal or administrative requirements.

YOUR CHOICES

You may unsubscribe from our marketing emails at any time. You may also contact us if you have a question about personal information you previously provided to STARGIRLS or would like to request that we review, correct or delete information where applicable.

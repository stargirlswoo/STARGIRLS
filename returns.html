RETURNS + REFUNDS

Last updated September 6, 2026.

Standard clothing returns

Unless a product page says otherwise, eligible stocked clothing may be requested for return within 14 days after delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, unused, with original tags or packaging where applicable, and in resellable condition.

Made-to-order merch

Made-to-order or print-on-demand merchandise is generally final sale because it is produced for your order. We will still review claims involving manufacturing defects, incorrect items, or damage in transit.

Beauty and fragrance

For hygiene and safety reasons, opened, used, unsealed or sampled beauty and fragrance products are final sale unless defective or incorrect. Unopened items may be eligible for return where permitted and where the product page does not state otherwise.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Contact us within 7 days of delivery through the official STARGIRLS website or social channels. Include your order information and clear photos where relevant so we can review the issue.

Final sale and preorder items

Items clearly marked FINAL SALE are not eligible for return unless defective, damaged or incorrect. Cancellation or refund rules for preorders may vary by product and will be disclosed on the product page before purchase.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected when a physical return is required. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to post the refund.

Original shipping charges

Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the return results from our error or applicable law requires otherwise. Return shipping responsibility will be stated when a return is approved.

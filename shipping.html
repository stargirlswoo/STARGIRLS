GET THE STUFF TO YOU.Last updated September 6, 2026. Product-specific processing and delivery information shown on product pages or at checkout controls when it differs from this page.
01

PROCESSING

Processing times vary by product and will be shown on the relevant product page or at checkout when available. Made-to-order merchandise, preorders, stocked clothing and beauty products may have different processing timelines.

02

MORE THAN ONE BOX?

Your order may arrive in more than one package when items are fulfilled from different locations or by different fulfillment partners. Separate packages do not necessarily mean anything is missing from your order.

03

RATES + ETA

Available shipping methods, charges and delivery estimates are shown at checkout. Delivery estimates begin after an order has finished processing and are not guaranteed unless the selected carrier service expressly states otherwise.

04

PREORDERS

Estimated preorder or launch dates are estimates and may change because of manufacturing, inbound freight, carrier delays or other circumstances outside our control. If a significant delay affects an order, we will use the contact information provided at checkout when possible.

05

CHECK. YOUR. ADDRESS.

Please review your shipping address before submitting an order. We cannot guarantee that an address can be changed after fulfillment begins. Additional shipping charges caused by an incorrect or incomplete address may be the customer's responsibility.

06

THE PACKAGE VANISHED?

If your package arrives damaged, is materially incomplete, or has a carrier issue, contact us promptly through the official STARGIRLS website or social channels and include your order information. Carrier investigations may be required before a replacement or other resolution can be provided.

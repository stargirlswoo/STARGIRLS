PROCESSING

Processing times vary by product. Made-to-order merchandise is produced before it ships, and available estimates are shown at checkout.

MORE THAN ONE BOX?

Your order may arrive in more than one package when items are fulfilled separately. Separate packages do not necessarily mean anything is missing.

03

RATES + ETA

Available shipping methods, charges and delivery estimates are shown at checkout. Delivery estimates begin after processing.

04

CHECK YOUR ADDRESS.

Please review your shipping address before submitting an order. We cannot guarantee an address can be changed after fulfillment begins.

05

PACKAGE MISSING?

Check your tracking and delivery location first. If an order arrives damaged, materially incomplete, or has a carrier issue, contact STARGIRLS support with your order information.

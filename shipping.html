THE IMPORTANT PART

GET THE STUFF TO YOU.Last updated September 9, 2026. Product-specific information and the shipping options displayed at checkout control when they differ from general estimates on this page.
01

MADE FOR YOUR ORDER

STARGIRLS apparel may be made to order. Production happens before shipping, so processing time and transit time are separate. Estimates shown at checkout are estimates unless a carrier expressly guarantees a service.

02

CHECK YOUR ADDRESS.

You are responsible for providing a complete and accurate shipping address at checkout. Review it before paying. Once production or fulfillment begins, we cannot guarantee that an address can be changed, intercepted, or redirected. Costs caused by an incorrect or incomplete customer-provided address may be the customer’s responsibility to the extent permitted by law.

03

TRACKING IS YOUR FRIEND.

When tracking is available, it is sent using the contact information provided with the order. Carrier scans and estimated delivery dates can change while a package is in transit. Please monitor tracking so you know when delivery is expected.

04

MORE THAN ONE BOX?

Items may ship separately when they are produced or fulfilled from different locations. A partial delivery does not necessarily mean the rest of the order is missing. Check all tracking numbers associated with the order before submitting a missing-item claim.

05

DELIVERED BUT MISSING?

If carrier tracking shows delivered but you cannot locate the package, first check the delivery location, household members, neighbors where appropriate, building staff, and the carrier. Report the issue through STARGIRLS Help + Support promptly. We may require order information, tracking details, confirmation of the shipping address, or other information reasonably needed to investigate. A carrier “delivered” scan does not automatically qualify a claim for an immediate refund; claims are reviewed based on the available evidence and applicable law.

06

DAMAGED PACKAGE?

Keep the merchandise, shipping label, and packaging and photograph them before discarding anything. Report material shipping damage within 7 days after delivery so the issue can be reviewed. We may require photographs or carrier information to verify the claim.

07

DELAYS HAPPEN.

Weather, carrier disruptions, customs, high-volume periods, incorrect addresses, and other events outside our reasonable control may delay delivery. A delay alone does not automatically cancel a valid order or create a right to an immediate refund, except where required by applicable law.

08

FRAUD + ABUSE

We may review or investigate claims involving inconsistent information, repeated missing-package reports, altered evidence, duplicate refund requests, or other reasonable indicators of fraud or abuse. We may also cancel or decline orders where our payment or fraud screening identifies material risk. Nothing on this page limits consumer rights that cannot legally be waived.

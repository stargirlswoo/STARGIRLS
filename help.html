ORDERS

When am I charged?

Your payment is processed through secure checkout when you place your order.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Orders can move into production quickly. If something is wrong, contact us as soon as possible. We cannot guarantee changes after fulfillment begins.

Where is my order confirmation?

Check the email address used at checkout, including spam or promotions folders.

SHIPPING

When will my order ship?

STARGIRLS apparel is made to order. Production happens before shipping, and available delivery estimates are shown during checkout.

How do I track my order?

When tracking becomes available, use the tracking information sent to the email address on your order.

My tracking says delivered, but I don't have it.

Check around the delivery location and with household members or neighbors first. If it still cannot be found, use your carrier tracking details and contact support.

RETURNS

Can I return something?

Eligibility depends on the reason for the return and the condition of the item. Read the full Returns + Refunds policy before sending anything back.

What if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Keep the item and packaging and document the issue with clear photos so the order can be reviewed.

When will I receive a refund?

Approved refunds can take additional time to appear after processing depending on your payment provider.

For complete order rules, use the policies below. Product and checkout information takes priority over general estimates.
